## PATH 1: HOW TO LAUNCH LIVE & START GETTING TRADERS

### Step 1: Connect the Payment & Banking Pipes (The Engine)
1. **Nigeria (NGN Payouts):**
   - Register a merchant account on **Paystack** (Transfers API), **Monnify**, or **Korapay**.
   - These APIs connect directly to NIBSS (Nigeria Inter-Bank Settlement System), crediting any Nigerian bank account in under 15 seconds.
2. **Ghana (GHS Payouts):**
   - Use **Paystack Mobile Money Transfers API** or **Flutterwave** for instant MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo payouts.
3. **Working Float Capital:**
   - Deposit a starter float of ₦2,000,000 – ₦5,000,000 in your merchant disbursement wallet so payments clear instantaneously with zero delay.

### Step 2: Establish Wholesale Gift Card Off-Ramp Desks
1. **The OTC Arbitrage Mechanics:**
   - When a trader sells you a $100 Steam or Apple card on Rubby at ₦1,250/$ (you pay ₦125,000 NGN).
   - You offload that verified card in bulk to wholesale Chinese, European, or US secondary market redemption syndicates at ₦1,340/$ (you receive ₦134,000 NGN).
   - **Your net profit: ₦9,000 NGN (~$6.00 USD) on a single transaction in 3 minutes.**
2. **Where to Find Liquidity Buyers:**
   - Institutional OTC desks on **Paxful** and **Noones**.
   - Direct bulk gift card redemption desks on WeChat/Telegram (deal only with verified escrow or collateralized desks).
   - Retailer liquidation services (e.g., US bulk electronic retail syndicates).

### Step 3: Anti-Fraud & Risk Management Safeguards
1. **Sub-5 Minute SLA Desk:** A trained operator checks card balance on official portal (Apple/Steam/Razer) within 3 minutes before hitting "Approve Payout".
2. **Receipt Hashing:** Barcode and receipt image hashing prevent traders from submitting the same card across multiple accounts.
3. **Blacklist Sync:** Block fraudulent device IDs, IP proxies, and compromised phone numbers.

### Step 4: High-Velocity Trader Acquisition Blitz (0 to 10,000 Traders)
1. **The ₦1,000 First-Trade Cash Incentive:** Credit ₦1,000 into every verified trader's wallet on their first trade of $50+. Card traders are yield-driven and test immediately.
2. **WhatsApp & Telegram VIP Desk:** Create a direct "Rubby VIP Desk" WhatsApp link for high-volume traders doing $500+/day, offering them +₦10/$ extra rate.
3. **Campus Ambassador Network:** Recruit 10 campus tech leads across UNILAG, UNN, University of Ghana, and KNUST with custom referral links paying 0.5% commission on all volume referred.
